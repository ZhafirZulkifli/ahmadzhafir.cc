Guiding Principle

A Perspective on Time & Mortality

عَنْ ابْن عُمَرَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُمَا قَالَ: أَخَذَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ بِمَنْكِبِي، وَقَالَ: «كُنْ فِي الدُّنْيَا كَأَنَّك غَرِيبٌ أَوْ عَابِرُ سَبِيلٍ». وَكَانَ ابْنُ عُمَرَ يَقُولُ: «إذَا أَمْسَيْتَ فَلَا تَنْتَظِرْ الصَّبَاحَ، وَإِذَا أَصْبَحْتَ فَلَا تَنْتَظِرْ الْمَسَاءَ، وَخُذْ مِنْ صِحَّتِك لِمَرَضِك، وَمِنْ حَيَاتِك لِمَوْتِك».
“On the authority of Abdullah ibn Umar: The Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) took me by the shoulder and said: ‘Be in this world as though you were a stranger or a wayfarer.’ And Ibn Umar used to say: ‘In the evening do not expect [to live until] the morning, and in the morning do not expect [to live until] the evening. Take advantage of your health before times of sickness, and of your life before your death.’”
— Sahih al-Bukhari

Dedicated to family, friends, colleagues, and all visitors. In a field dedicated to safeguarding health and preserving life, these words serve as an enduring reminder of service, humility, and purpose.
